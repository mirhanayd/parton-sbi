# ADR-001: continuous PDF family

Status: Accepted for staged validation

Date: 2026-07-28

## Context

Phase 1A rejected discrete-member nominal-pool reuse because its predeclared
ESS gate failed. The next candidate family must support direct generation,
preserve proton sum rules, remain small enough for an initial identifiability
study, and avoid claims of unrestricted flavor separation from inclusive
neutral-current electron-proton data.

Two approaches were considered:

1. a multiplicative deformation of CT18NLO member 0;
2. an independent analytic input basis.

The analytic basis would require new choices for sea asymmetry, strange
fraction, gluon shape, endpoint powers, and threshold matching before the
direct-generation machinery itself is validated.

## Decision

Use a two-parameter multiplicative deformation of CT18NLO member 0 at the
metadata-derived lower grid scale. For CT18NLO `DataVersion: 1`, that scale is
`Q0 = 1.295 GeV`.

The parameters are:

```text
theta = (delta_v, lambda_sea).
```

`delta_v` applies a common valence tilt. Separate normalizations impose
`integral(u_v)=2` and `integral(d_v)=1`. `lambda_sea` scales the common light
sea exponentially while preserving baseline flavor ratios. A positive gluon
normalization imposes the momentum sum rule. Heavy flavors are zero at the
verified boundary and are generated by APFEL++ variable-flavor evolution.

The family has compact metadata-derived support and no extrapolation.
Positivity is a gate; negative values are not clipped.

The roadmap box `delta_v in [-0.20,0.20]`,
`lambda_sea in [-0.25,0.25]` is only a candidate pilot box. It does not become
a production prior until the complete Stage 0 scan passes.

## Consequences

- The family locally explores an established NLO baseline and retains its
  detailed flavor structure.
- It is intentionally not a general PDF fit and cannot justify full-flavor
  separation claims.
- Exact sum-rule and metadata handling become mandatory implementation gates.
- The design is initially coupled to a named baseline and data version.
- A failed positivity, quadrature, or pilot-box test is a valid negative
  result and requires a new scientific decision.

## Rejected and deferred alternatives

- An independent analytic basis is rejected for the MVP.
- Independent valence, sea-flavor, strange, and gluon-shape parameters are
  deferred.
- Silently moving `Q0` from the actual lower grid boundary is rejected.
- Extrapolation and positivity repair are rejected.

## Validation

The binding Stage 0 and Stage 1 criteria are in
`../AMORTIZED_INFERENCE_PHASE1BD_ACCEPTANCE.md`. This ADR authorizes design
review only; it does not authorize implementation beyond a separately scoped
Phase 1B-D0 task.

## Revisit conditions

Revisit this decision only if Stage 0 rejects the pilot family, Stage 1 cannot
reconstruct/evolve the central baseline within tolerance, or additional
approved scattering channels create a scientifically supported need for new
flavor directions. A failed test must be documented before an alternative
family is selected.
