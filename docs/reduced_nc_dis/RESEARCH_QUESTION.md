# Research question and paper boundary

## Unchanged high-level objective

> Can continuous PDF deformations be constrained from unbinned DIS event sets
> using amortized simulation-based inference?

The inference unit is a set of events,

```text
D = {y_1, ..., y_N}
target = p(theta_PDF | D)
```

The objective is not to determine an instantaneous PDF of one proton from one
event. Inclusive neutral-current electron-proton data do not establish
unrestricted full-flavor PDF separation.

## Operational Phase 2 paper question

> Can amortized simulation-based inference produce calibrated posterior
> constraints on continuous PDF deformations from unbinned neutral-current DIS
> event sets generated by an explicit, normalized, detector-aware reduced
> observation model?

The latent phase-space region `A_z`, complete detector output space `Y_full`,
and selected observed-event region `Y_obs` are distinct. The full detector
kernel and selected fixed-N baseline are

```text
z_i ~ p_theta(z)

p_theta(z) =
  1_{A_z}(z) [d sigma_theta / dz]
  / integral_{A_z} [d sigma_theta / dz] dz

K_full(dy_star | z) >= 0
integral_{Y_full} K_full(dy_star | z) = 1

epsilon(z) = K_full(Y_obs | z)
alpha_theta = integral_{A_z} p_theta(z) epsilon(z) dz

q_theta(dy | selected) =
  1_{Y_obs}(y)
  integral_{A_z} p_theta(z) K_full(dy | z) dz
  / alpha_theta

D = {y_1, ..., y_N}
y_i iid/exchangeable from q_theta(y | selected)
p(D | theta, N, selected) = product_i q_theta(y_i | selected)
```

`BASELINE_SELECTION_MODE = SELECTED_EVENT_CONDITIONED_V1`. The planned minimum
viable study conditions on `N` and selection and uses the normalized selected
shape law. `alpha_theta` is load-bearing whenever efficiency is not identically
one. Selection-induced shape dependence is included, but total selected-count
information and rejected/null outcomes are excluded from `D`. A null outcome
is part of `Y_full` whenever needed to preserve detector probability mass.
Lossless response has `epsilon(z)=1`; a compatible perfect-detector limit must
recover the declared latent law. Phase 2H is the only optional count/rate
extension and requires a separate observation law, luminosity, and
normalization semantics.

## Identifiability and information content

Posterior correctness and posterior informativeness are separate. Define
observational equivalence by

```text
theta ~ theta_prime
iff
q_theta(D | N, selected) = q_theta_prime(D | N, selected)
on the declared observation space.
```

Phase 2A must bind the theta domain and prior, reported parameter combinations,
potentially non-identifiable directions, prior-dominated posterior diagnostics,
degeneracy reporting, and a bounded falsifiable later information-content
plan. Candidate later diagnostics may include information gain, predeclared
posterior contraction, prior-predictive or anchor distinguishability,
log-score improvement, parameter-combination resolution, or likelihood-ratio
and classifier distinguishability. No metric is selected here, and no
information-content experiment is authorized or executed. Calibration and
coverage do not by themselves establish that the data are informative about
theta.

## Proof-of-principle claim boundary

If later validated, a methodology paper may claim:

- set-level amortized inference;
- continuous PDF deformation inference;
- an explicit normalized NC DIS observation law;
- unbinned event-set inference;
- detector-aware inference through a normalized kernel;
- posterior calibration and repeated-sampling coverage; and
- proof-of-principle sensitivity only for predeclared parameter combinations
  that pass the later identifiability and information-content gates.

It must not claim:

- full PYTHIA equivalence;
- showering or initial-state radiation;
- hadronization, beam-remnant, or underlying-event modelling;
- complete collider-event realism;
- production-grade detector simulation;
- unrestricted full-flavor PDF determination;
- replacement of a global PDF fit;
- legacy D2 completion; or
- full-generator closure;
- universal identifiability of all PDF-deformation parameters; or
- guaranteed posterior contraction for every permitted theta direction.

Hard flavor, `GenPdfInfo`, and other generator-only truth remain provenance or
reweighting truth. They are not default observed ML features.
