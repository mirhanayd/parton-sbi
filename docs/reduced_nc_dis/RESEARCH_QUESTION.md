# Research question and paper boundary

## Unchanged high-level objective

> Can continuous PDF deformations be constrained from unbinned DIS event sets
> using amortized simulation-based inference?

The inference unit is a set of events,

```text
D = {y_1, ..., y_N}
target = p(theta_PDF | D)
```

The objective is not to determine an instantaneous PDF of one proton from one
event. Inclusive neutral-current electron-proton data do not establish
unrestricted full-flavor PDF separation.

## Operational Phase 2 paper question

> Can amortized simulation-based inference produce calibrated posterior
> constraints on continuous PDF deformations from unbinned neutral-current DIS
> event sets generated by an explicit, normalized, detector-aware reduced
> observation model?

The baseline law is

```text
z_i ~ p_theta(z)
y_i ~ K(y | z_i)
D = {y_i}_{i=1}^N

p_theta(z) =
  1_A(z) [d sigma_theta / dz]
  / integral_A [d sigma_theta / dz] dz
```

The planned minimum viable study is fixed-N, shape-only, unbinned, set-level,
detector-aware amortized posterior inference. A later count/rate extension is
optional and requires a separate observation law, luminosity, and normalization
semantics.

## Proof-of-principle claim boundary

If later validated, a methodology paper may claim:

- set-level amortized inference;
- continuous PDF deformation inference;
- an explicit normalized NC DIS observation law;
- unbinned event-set inference;
- detector-aware inference through a normalized kernel;
- posterior calibration and repeated-sampling coverage; and
- proof-of-principle sensitivity under the declared reduced model.

It must not claim:

- full PYTHIA equivalence;
- showering or initial-state radiation;
- hadronization, beam-remnant, or underlying-event modelling;
- complete collider-event realism;
- production-grade detector simulation;
- unrestricted full-flavor PDF determination;
- replacement of a global PDF fit;
- legacy D2 completion; or
- full-generator closure.

Hard flavor, `GenPdfInfo`, and other generator-only truth remain provenance or
reweighting truth. They are not default observed ML features.
